Membership Terms. Standard membership is billed monthly and includes unrestricted gym floor access, all group classes, and locker use during visits. Off-peak membership is billed at a reduced rate and permits entry between 10 AM and 4 PM on weekdays only. Annual membership is paid upfront and includes two guest passes per month.

Cancellation. Monthly memberships require 30 days' written notice, submitted at reception or by email. Annual memberships are non-refundable except on documented medical grounds, in which case the unused balance is refunded pro rata. Freezing a membership is permitted once per year for up to 3 months at a nominal holding fee.

Facility Rules. Members must wipe down equipment after use and re-rack all weights. Personal training may only be conducted by staff trainers. The pool and sauna require a separate access wristband issued at reception.
