Corner Roastery Menu and Hours. The cafe opens at 7 AM on weekdays and 8 AM on weekends, closing at 9 PM daily. The kitchen stops taking hot food orders 45 minutes before closing.

Coffee. We serve single-origin espresso from rotating Ethiopian, Colombian, and Kenyan lots, roasted weekly in house. Filter coffee is brewed to order using V60 or Chemex. Oat, almond, and soy milk are available at no extra charge.

Food. The all-day breakfast menu includes shakshuka, avocado toast, and buttermilk pancakes. Sandwiches and salads are served from 11 AM. The pastry case is restocked twice daily at 7 AM and 2 PM, and anything left after 8:30 PM is sold at half price.

Seating and Wi-Fi. The cafe has 40 covers indoors and 16 on the terrace. Free Wi-Fi is available; during weekend brunch service, laptop use is limited to the terrace and the back room.
