Borrowing Rules. Members may borrow up to 6 items at a time. General collection books circulate for 21 days and may be renewed twice unless another member has placed a hold. Reference materials, bound periodicals, and rare collection items do not circulate and must be consulted in the reading room.

Overdue Items. Overdue items accrue a small daily charge per item, capped at the replacement cost of the item. Borrowing privileges are suspended once outstanding charges exceed the threshold set by the circulation desk. Lost items are billed at replacement cost plus a processing fee.

Reading Room. The reading room is open during staffed hours and requires a valid member card for entry. Bags larger than a small handbag must be left in the lockers. Food and covered drinks are not permitted beyond the lobby.
