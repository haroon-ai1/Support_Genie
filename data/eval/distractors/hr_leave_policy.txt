Employee Leave Policy. Full-time employees accrue 20 days of paid annual leave per calendar year, accruing monthly from the date of joining. Unused leave of up to 5 days may be carried into the following year and must be used by 31 March. Sick leave is granted for 10 days per year and requires a medical certificate for absences longer than two consecutive days. Casual leave of 8 days per year covers short personal absences and cannot be combined with annual leave.

Leave Approval. All leave requests are submitted through the HR portal at least 5 working days in advance, except in emergencies. Line managers approve or decline within 2 working days. Leave during the December closing period requires additional approval from the department head, as finance and operations run at reduced capacity.
